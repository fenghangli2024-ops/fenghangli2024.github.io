## 欢迎 / Welcome

I am a Ph.D. student (2024 cohort) at the School of Public Policy and Management, Tsinghua University. My research interests include social security, health economics, and public policy evaluation, with a focus on the effects of long-term care insurance, intergenerational mobility, and household resource allocation.

## 当前研究 / Current Research

我的主要研究项目评估中国长期护理保险试点对老年人心理健康的因果影响，并考察该政策的代际福利外溢——对成年子女劳动供给和心理健康、以及学龄期孙辈教育参与的影响。研究采用多期双重差分（staggered DID）和事件研究法，结合 CFPS 与 CHARLS 微观数据，处理异质性处理效应，并从正式照护可及性、家庭消费与转移支付、劳动供给等渠道解释机制。

My primary research project evaluates the causal effects of China's long-term care insurance pilots on elderly mental health, and examines intergenerational welfare spillovers — on adult children's labor supply and mental health, and on school-age grandchildren's educational engagement. The study employs staggered difference-in-differences and event study methods, using CFPS and CHARLS micro data, accounting for heterogeneous treatment effects, and exploring mechanisms through formal care access, household consumption and transfers, and labor supply adjustments.
