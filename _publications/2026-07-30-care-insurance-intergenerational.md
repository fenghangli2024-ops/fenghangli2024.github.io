## 摘要 / Abstract

本研究评估中国长期护理保险试点对老年人心理健康的因果影响，并考察该政策的代际福利外溢——对成年子女劳动供给和心理健康、以及学龄期孙辈教育参与的影响。研究采用多期双重差分法（staggered DID）和事件研究法，结合 CFPS 与 CHARLS 微观调查数据，处理异质性处理效应，并通过正式照护可及性、家庭消费与经济转移支付、劳动供给调整等渠道解释作用机制。

This study evaluates the causal effects of China's long-term care insurance pilots on elderly mental health and examines intergenerational welfare spillovers — on adult children's labor supply and mental health, and on school-age grandchildren's educational engagement. Using staggered difference-in-differences and event study methods with CFPS and CHARLS micro data, the analysis accounts for heterogeneous treatment effects and explores mechanisms through formal care access, household consumption and transfers, and labor supply adjustments.
